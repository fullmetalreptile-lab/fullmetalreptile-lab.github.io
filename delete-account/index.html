Delete your account

Applies to From Dust We Came, Sovereign Horizon and Mommy Milker Monster Hunters

You can ask us to delete your account and the data tied to it at any time.

How to request it

  1. Email fullmetalreptile@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account".
  2. Tell us which game(s) and your in-game display name. If you can, include the platform you play on (Steam, Android).
  3. We'll confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Your player profile, progress, stats, loadouts, inventory, friends and clan membership.

What we may keep

Purchase and refund records that tax or accounting law requires us to keep (up to 7 years), and records of bans for cheating or abuse, so banned players can't simply start over. Chat messages other players already received stay on their devices.

Deleting your account can't be undone, and items or currency on it are lost. Your Steam, Google or Epic account itself isn't affected; manage those with each company.
