About From Dust We Came

A new map every match

Maps are generated fresh for each match, so every round starts with everyone on equal footing.

PvP and co-op

Go head to head in PvP or team up with friends for co-op missions.

Progression that counts

Profile levels, weapon mastery, daily quests and career challenges, so every match moves you forward.

Made to be streamed

Streaming, videos and screenshots are welcome, including on monetized channels.

Join the playtest

  1. Open the From Dust We Came Steam page.
  2. Click Request Access. You'll be added to the pool.
  3. Players are let in in batches from Oct 30 to Nov 15, so watch your Steam notifications.
  4. Found a bug? Use REPORT A BUG in Settings or the Escape menu. It comes straight to the developer.

FAQ

What is FDWC?
FDWC is short for From Dust We Came, a multiplayer FPS by Full Metal Reptile where every match is played on a freshly generated map.
How do I join the playtest?
Open the Steam page and click Request Access. The playtest runs from October 30 to November 15, 2026.
Can I stream it?
Yes. Streaming, videos and screenshots are welcome, including on monetized channels. Please mention it's a playtest build.
Which platforms?
Windows PC, through Steam.
Who makes it?
From Dust We Came is developed by Full Metal Reptile and published by Grudge Studio.
