Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

This policy explains what information Full Metal Reptile ("we", "us") collects when you play our games (From Dust We Came, Sovereign Horizon and Mommy Milker Monster Hunters) on Steam, Google Play or any other store, and how we use it. Questions: fullmetalreptile@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account identifiers. To let you play online, the games sign you in through your platform account or through Epic Online Services (EOS). This gives us a player ID, your display name and, on PC, your Steam ID. If you don't link an Epic account, EOS creates an anonymous ID tied to your device.
  • Game data. Your progress, levels, stats, loadouts, unlocked and purchased in-game items, settings and match results, so your progress is saved and matches work.
  • Purchase records. When you buy something, the store (Steam or Google Play) handles the payment. We receive an order or transaction ID, the item, price, currency and status so we can deliver the item and handle refunds. We never see your card or bank details.
  • Chat and voice. Text chat and voice chat are sent in real time to other players in your lobby or match. We don't record voice chat.
  • Reports and moderation. If you report a player, or someone reports you, we keep the report (who, which category, when) and any action taken, such as a ban, to keep the games fair and safe.
  • Technical data. Your IP address is needed to connect you to matches and servers. The games may also send basic device information (device model, operating system, game version) and error or crash logs so we can fix bugs.

We don't collect your precise location, contacts, photos or microphone recordings, and we don't sell your personal information.

How we use it

  • To run online play, matchmaking, parties and clans.
  • To save your progress and deliver purchases.
  • To prevent cheating, fraud and abuse, and to enforce our Terms.
  • To answer support requests and fix problems.

Who we share it with

Only the services that run the games for us, and only what they need: Valve (Steam), Google (Google Play and Google Play Billing), Epic Games (Epic Online Services for sign-in, matchmaking, voice, reports and moderation) and Cloudflare (hosting for our purchase server). Other players can see your display name, profile, stats and anything you say in chat. We may also share information if the law requires it.

Ads

The games don't show ads right now. If we add optional ads in the future, we'll update this policy first.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and game data while your account is active. Purchase records may be kept longer where tax or accounting law requires. Moderation records are kept as long as needed to keep the games safe.

Your choices and rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account and data. See Delete your account, or email us. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have further rights, and we'll honor them.

Children

Our games aren't made for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

We use the security of the platforms above and keep access to player data limited. No system is perfect, so we can't promise absolute security.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for big changes, tell players in the games or on our channels.
